# ifrs_treasury_review_agent: document stewardship

Accountable role: IFRS::Treasury and Currency Accounting Lead

Reviewer: IFRS::Financial Reporting and Disclosure Lead

Motivation: maintain correct, current, reconcilable evidence and reporting assets; expose unresolved issues before apparent completion.

## Borrowing cost expense and disclosure schedule (BORROWING-COST-SCHEDULE)

Why: Prevent ineligible capitalisation and reconcile financing expense to the disclosed amount.

Authority: required-reporting-content; exact title/container is a workflow choice. Source control references: IFRS25-25-C01, IFRS25-25-C02, IFRS25-25-C03, IFRS25-25-C04, IFRS25-25-C05.

Create/maintain: borrowings, interest, otherCosts, expenseRecognition, amountDisclosed, policy. Dependencies: INSTRUMENT-REGISTER, POLICY-REGISTER.

On period/evidence/fact/policy/source/review changes, reassess applicability, request missing authentic evidence, draft/revise, reconcile, and obtain exact-revision independent review. Superseding an input invalidates dependent approvals. Never manufacture third-party evidence or silently replace approved content.
