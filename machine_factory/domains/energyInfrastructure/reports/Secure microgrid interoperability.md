# Build interoperable microgrids with verified authority

## Current acceptance scope

The [owner-approved acceptance scope](../acceptance/scope.json) governs this active document: **all required hardware is simulated**, and **template/generic assets may be used in every case**. EI04 must provide templates for each asset class defined by the associated applicable standards, with edition/clause traceability; EI18 must verify a simulated counterpart for every hardware dependency. Generic reference parameters replace site-specific prerequisites for corpus admission. Actual software execution and genuine admission/reviewer evidence remain mandatory. Physical equipment certification and field commissioning are separate future activities. Historical research notes retain their original research context and do not override this decision.

The new domain should combine **engineered local protection, deterministic supervisory control, tested protocol profiles, and tightly scoped model assistance**. NIST SP 1108r4 provides the organizing framework; it is not a controller specification or a current list of every governing standard. This factory package now contains the standards, implementation, risk, RAG and acceptance inventories needed to make the design reviewable. The existing 187 energy definitions remain unchanged as provenance, and the supplied NIST PDF is the only newly collected reference. Secure communications are a prerequisite, while trustworthy observations, authorized operations and safe electrical behavior require separate controls and evidence. **No new-domain runtime, RAG index, field integration, native quorum test, hardware conformance or admission has been completed.** The proposed baseline is US IEEE/NIST with selected international IEC profiles; utility, jurisdiction, AHJ and actual equipment remain decisive inputs.

## Interoperability belongs to tested profiles and operating modes

NIST's conceptual domains span customers, markets, service providers, operations, generation including DER, transmission and distribution. Its microgrid communication view includes customer- and utility-managed systems and cross-owner responsibilities. Those distinctions matter because a utility service, a site EMS and an inverter do not acquire the same operating authority merely by exchanging messages. The supplied February 2021 publication is 239 physical pages, SHA-256 `c1e6193af8f94b3f4fc48725a2e3c6eea497d9999cb968ecec2cb6be5d429c86`. Conceptual domains are in §2.1.2/Table 1, printed pp.15–16/physical pp.31–32; the microgrid view is §2.2.5/Figure 8, printed pp.24–25/physical pp.40–41. ([NIST publication](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1108r4), [local original](../intake/NIST.SP.1108r4_1.pdf))

The principal operational baseline is IEEE 1547/1547.1 for DER interconnection and equipment testing and IEEE 2030.7/2030.8 for microgrid controller functions and tests. The chosen utility interface should use its approved native IEEE 2030.5 profile where supported, or explicitly selected DNP3/IEEE 1815.2 where required. IEC 61850-7-420 supplies DER semantics, applicable 61850 station parts supply IED exchange, and selected IEC 61968/61970 CIM profiles carry topology and identifiers. SunSpec Modbus, OpenADR, OCPP and ISO 15118 apply to the actual DER, demand-response and EV cohorts. Time synchronization, calibrated power-quality measurement and storage electrical/fire safety need their own profiles and independently checked simulation evidence for corpus acceptance. The [46-entry inventory](../standards-inventory.json) records checked editions, applicability, sources and unresolved subsets rather than treating every alternative as mandatory. ([IEEE DER owner list](https://sagroups.ieee.org/scc21/ieee-1547-series/), [IEC DER model](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/34384), [OpenADR specifications](https://www.openadr.org/specification), [OCA](https://openchargealliance.org/faq/))

NIST explains how standards optionality can still yield incompatible implementations and why procurement must specify an interoperability profile. Its profile discussion is §6.5, printed pp.95–98/physical pp.111–114; procurement is §6.4.4, printed pp.94–95/physical pp.110–111. Accordingly each project interface must pin edition/amendments, protocol/encoding, functions/options, information model, units/sign/quality, ownership, trust/roles, timing, command precedence, state transitions, fallback and test evidence. A working library or certified component does not establish a compatible pair or safe system. [The profile template](../templates/interoperability-profile.json) and [DER example](../examples/der-supervisory-profile.json) make those requirements concrete without inventing site limits. ([NIST framework](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1108r4))

The lifecycle must cover grid-connected import/export and services; planned and unplanned islanding; island stabilization and reserve; engineered black start if supported; utility-authorized synch-check/reconnection; and measured service recovery. Independent relays, BMS and primary inverter control own fast electrical protection. The EMS enforces deterministic network, thermal, ramp, reserve and critical-load constraints. PE adapters validate raw evidence and RE evaluates normalized deterministic states. Models cannot supply missing protection limits, decide a breaker is safe to close, or establish that an action physically completed. Numerical thresholds and timing budgets remain **GENERIC_REFERENCE_PROFILE_REQUIRED**, tied to reviewed generic scenario parameters and applicable standards. [Architecture and lifecycle](../documents/workflow/architecture.md)

## Security must reassess evidence and constrain actual commands

The security baseline combines NISTIR 7628 relationship/interface analysis, final SP 800-82r3 OT engineering, CSF 2.0 governance, SP 800-30/37/53 assessment/control/authorization and current SP 800-61r3 incident guidance with IEC 62443 zones/conduits and IEC 62351 protocol-specific security. IEEE 1686 informs IED capability procurement. SP 800-82r4 is a 2026 initial public draft, not the final baseline. None of these labels means a configured system is compliant or safe. ([NISTIR 7628](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/7628/r1/final), [OT final guidance](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final), [draft watch](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/operational-technology-security/publications), [CSF](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20), [incident revision](https://csrc.nist.gov/News/2025/nist-revises-sp-800-61), [IEC security series](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/6912))

Secure channels authenticate communications but cannot make a compromised sensor truthful or an authorized command feasible. Preserve separate evidence-quality, cyber-posture and electrical-mode states. Reassess on threat, topology, firmware, policy/model/RAG, credential and freshness changes; correlate authenticated observations, clock/calibration quality, SBOM/VEX and actual exploitation context; record evidence hashes, rationale, uncertainty, treatment, owner and expiration. Missing/stale intelligence must not become low risk. MITRE ATT&CK ICS and CISA KEV inform scenarios and priorities, not automatic disconnection instructions. NIST's cybersecurity discussion, §5 printed pp.67–84/physical pp.83–100, warns that hasty isolation can itself cause serious physical consequences. ([ATT&CK ICS](https://attack.mitre.org/matrices/ics/), [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog), [NIST framework](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1108r4))

The [20-scenario risk inventory](../risk-register.json) addresses false telemetry, replay/time attacks, rogue masters, units/asset mistakes, approval-to-execution state changes, unsafe aggregate dispatch, key compromise, configuration/model supply chains, AI loss, unknown/forged completion, injection, resource exhaustion, confidentiality, physical hazards, misleading carbon and false admission. Before invocation the local deterministic command gate must verify principal/delegation, exact asset/action, fresh state and topology/config/policy revisions, feasibility, locks, approval-bound hash, expiry and replay/idempotency fencing. It records denial or bounded execution and independent readback. Cancellation and compensation are assessed against physical state; a breaker operation is not reversible by editing a ledger. Residual-risk acceptance and switching/protection authority remain with named authorized humans. [Dynamic security contract](../documents/workflow/security-and-dynamic-risk.md)

Model integration must preserve PE ACP/completion semantics and scoped document/evidence boundaries while adding actual field authorization. Agents have separate planning, review, execution and evidence roles. A model holds no field credentials and gets no general shell, arbitrary URL or actuator tool; typed outputs are untrusted until validated. NIST AI RMF/AI 600-1 and OWASP 2026 LLM/agentic guidance motivate groundedness, injection, tool/identity misuse, memory and supply-chain evaluations. Inference or retrieval failure holds dependent discretionary proposals; engineered local operation continues. [Agent/model contract](../documents/workflow/agent-model-integration.md), ([NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), [OWASP LLM](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-genai-llm-top-10-2026/), [OWASP agentic](https://genai.owasp.org/top-10-for-agentic-applications-2026/))

## RAG and carbon trajectories need provenance and accounting boundaries

The [RAG inventory](../rag-source-manifest.json) covers 18 source families: framework/normative profiles; security/AI guidance; utility/site agreements; as-built topology; OEM/firmware/certification records; protection/hazard studies; calibration/time/telemetry; approved operation/restoration procedures; threats/SBOM/VEX; model/tool provenance; current RE/PE contracts; carbon methods; regional operating signals; global context; actual approvals/readbacks; and isolated evaluations. Only NIST is received. Standards full texts, site data, provider terms and other documents remain to collect; no approved extract/index or retrieval validation exists. Public catalog visibility does not grant full-text indexing or model-training rights.

Source admission must bind exact bytes, edition/effective date, authenticity, jurisdiction/equipment applicability, rights, site/role/license scope, reviewer and expiry. Extraction retains page/clause/table lineage, definitions, exceptions and units. Filter permission before retrieval; keep confidential/licensed/threat/holdout material separated; return revision/page citations or abstain. Retrieved documents, tool results and memory are evidence, not instructions or grants of operating authority. Historical embeddings are not live telemetry or authenticated approvals. [RAG governance](../documents/workflow/rag-collection-and-governance.md)

Electricity carbon intensity must distinguish CO2 from CO2e, direct from lifecycle, production from consumption, average from marginal, and location from market accounting. Compute compatible interval emissions and divide by aligned delivered energy; weight by kWh rather than arithmetic-average unequal-energy intervals. Carry charging emissions through storage and losses, avoid charge/discharge or export double counting, and report absolute emissions and service delivered alongside intensity. A marginal impact estimate needs a counterfactual and uncertainty; a local low-carbon schedule does not prove a change in global average intensity. [Carbon contract](../documents/workflow/electricity-carbon-intensity.md), ([ElectricityMaps method](https://www.electricitymaps.com/data/methodology), [WattTime terms](https://watttime.org/noncommercial-agreement/), [GHG Scope 2](https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-2-guidance))

EIA/eGRID, ENTSO-E, ElectricityMaps and WattTime are regional/provider options whose rights, region, lag and purpose must be selected for provider integration; generic replay fixtures are permitted for acceptance; Ember and IEA are global context/scenario sources. Observed, forecast and approved target trajectories remain separate; no site target or provider connection is approved here. Carbon objectives never override protection, reserve or critical-service requirements. [Provider inventory](../rag-source-manifest.json)

## Acceptance requires new identities and several kinds of proof

The exact factory name `energyInfrastructure` is preserved, but production tools require lowercase kebab-case. A canonical slug/mapping decision, coexistence-versus-replacement decision and dispositions for all 187 inherited definitions precede runtime authoring. The clone's energy category/identities/lanes, placeholder governance and absent carbon model cannot simply be copied into an independently admitted namespace. Deployment tooling additionally needs explicit IDs and a JSON-only payload, while the historical README remains in the clone. These are concrete authoring/tooling tasks, not evidence that the currently accepted legacy energy corpus fails its loader schema. [Workplan](../WORKPLAN.md)

The ten phases progress through scope/authority, lawful sources, safety/profile design, candidate contracts, adapter/model integration, adversarial simulation with all required hardware simulated, canonical staging, official prepare, independent review/finalization and versioned software deployment/simulated commissioning. The 43 proposed cases bind operating, threat, carbon, RAG/model, transport, native parity, admission and recovery outcomes. All are NOT_RUN. Actual prepare requires a staged canonical nonaccepted candidate, clean committed protected inputs, required tools and a reviewed mandatory integration runner; its fresh scope boot resets services and deliberately omits OpenClaw/localAI. It therefore requires explicit future reset authorization and a real energy integration suite. C++, LSP and Scala provide native quorum evidence, while applicable Rust/Manager follower checks, wire profiles, simulated-hardware validation and software protocol tests answer different questions. TEST_ONLY cannot authorize production. [Cases](../acceptance/cases.json), [official implementation](https://github.com/jateeter/RealityEngine_Machines/blob/ebb75ee8ea34c13c86a5e3364eb83b550ad96693/scripts/domain-approval/approval.py)

The practical result is a reviewable specification and evidence plan, not an assertion of complete security or universal interoperability. Admission becomes credible when each selected capability has an accountable owner, lawful authoritative sources, reproducible artifacts and measured outcomes at the correct test level. Keeping the historical clone, documentary authority, runtime decisions and physical completion separate allows future improvements without letting model confidence or a static checksum masquerade as plant assurance.
