# Security, dynamic threat assessment and risk control

## Current acceptance scope

The [owner-approved acceptance scope](../../acceptance/scope.json) governs this active document: **all required hardware is simulated**, and **template/generic assets may be used in every case**. EI04 must provide templates for each asset class defined by the associated applicable standards, with edition/clause traceability; EI18 must verify a simulated counterpart for every hardware dependency. Generic reference parameters replace site-specific prerequisites for corpus admission. Actual software execution and genuine admission/reviewer evidence remain mandatory. **DESIGN_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_ENFORCEMENT_EVIDENCE** · All channels are assumed secure as a domain entry prerequisite. This assumption does not establish current wire state, endpoint trustworthiness, authorization, data truth or safe actuation.

## Tailor a layered security baseline

NISTIR 7628r1 organizes smart-grid relationship/interface risk; SP 800-82r3 is the final OT-security baseline checked on 24 November 2026; r4 is an initial public draft and belongs on change watch. CSF 2.0 supplies governance/outcome profiles; SP 800-30/37/53 support assessment/control/authorization; SP 800-61r3 covers current incident guidance; SP 800-207 supports resource-specific authorization. IEC 62443 addresses asset-owner/integrator responsibilities, zones/conduits, risk-derived target security levels and system/component assurance. IEC 62351 parts must be selected against the actual protocol; IEEE 1686 supplies IED capability criteria. These frameworks do not certify the package. [NISTIR 7628](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/7628/r1/final), [OT final guidance](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final), [OT draft watch](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/operational-technology-security/publications), [CSF 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20), [incident guidance](https://csrc.nist.gov/News/2025/nist-revises-sp-800-61), [IEC 62351](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/6912).

Define protection/IED, DER/BMS, supervisory EMS, OT DMZ/gateway, utility/enterprise, AI/RAG and maintenance/security zones. Assess target security levels by zone/conduit; do not infer an IEC security level from encryption alone. Each conduit records its owner, peers, identities, profile, direction, functions, policy, latency/freshness, failure behavior, trust lifecycle and measured secure-channel evidence.

Internal repository service links follow the current [CI transport profile](https://github.com/jateeter/RealityEngine_CI/blob/c985c0d7b592013d4d830b079d5af1663d407b6c/config/transport-profile.json): TLS 1.3, selected AEAD, hybrid-group preference and service mTLS with installation CA, subject to explicit link-specific owner dispositions. Field GOOSE/SV and native OPC UA are not all TLS sockets; IEC 62351 transport profiles can permit versions the internal policy disallows. Resolve this at a reviewed gateway/trust boundary or through an explicit scoped owner disposition; tunneling does not authenticate a compromised field endpoint or prove native protocol conformance. No plaintext fallback or disabled certificate verification is authorized.

Inspected source records existing exceptions for OpenClaw inbound TLS/bearer/loopback, the Qdrant listener and CSS client-certificate limitations. PIM→CSS remains TLS 1.3 without mTLS, compensated by Solid-OIDC/DPoP and loopback restriction. These are source-only observations, not current probes or transferable exemptions. Reuse requires current link review and wire evidence; do not relabel them mTLS. See [CI transport design](https://github.com/jateeter/RealityEngine_CI/blob/c985c0d7b592013d4d830b079d5af1663d407b6c/docs/TRANSPORT_SECURITY.md) and its [probe implementation](https://github.com/jateeter/RealityEngine_CI/blob/c985c0d7b592013d4d830b079d5af1663d407b6c/scripts/transport-probe.py).

## Assess risk continuously without letting uncertainty become safe

The [risk inventory](../../risk-register.json) records 20 scenarios with controls, owners, fallback and acceptance links. Initial and residual risks remain unassessed/unaccepted until site analysis. Use scenario-specific likelihood, consequences, evidence, uncertainty and expiry; assess human safety, protection integrity, service continuity, privacy and environmental impact separately. No arbitrary score multiplication establishes compliance. A named human authority accepts residual risk with scope and expiration; model prose cannot accept it.

1. Ingest authenticated allowlisted observations, asset/config/SBOM/VEX state, trust events, adapter audits, passive-network alerts, maintenance, weather/hazards and pinned threat feeds.
2. Validate identity, schema, units, sequence, event/receipt age, calibration, provenance and clock quality. Mark missing, stale, conflicting and synthetic evidence explicitly.
3. Correlate detections with asset reachability, operating mode, exploit evidence, firmware and critical-load consequences. KEV absence or stale intelligence cannot lower risk by itself.
4. Produce a reproducible assessment record with scenario, evidence hashes, policy/version, rationale, confidence, control status, proposed treatment and reassessment deadline.
5. Evaluate independent `evidenceState` (valid/degraded/unknown/conflicting), `cyberPosture` (normal/suspect/restricted/incident) and plant mode. The deterministic policy returns allow, boundedAllow, holdForReview or block; hard electrical/safety inhibits prevail.
6. Revoke compromised capabilities or quarantine observation channels through approved mode-specific procedures. Cyber containment must not blindly disconnect critical services. Retain independent protection and reconcile field state.
7. Escalate to operations, utility and OT security; preserve evidence; restore trusted configuration/credentials; verify physical topology and service recovery; obtain independent closeout before reopening automation.
8. Reassess on topology/mode, threat, firmware, credential, model/policy/RAG changes, evidence timeouts and scheduled reviews. Apply approved hysteresis and budgets to avoid alert-driven oscillation.

MITRE ATT&CK ICS guides threat scenarios, STIX/TAXII structured feeds, and CISA KEV plus CVE/VEX exploitation prioritization. They are evidence sources, not instructions to operate equipment or quantitative risk calculators. [ATT&CK ICS](https://attack.mitre.org/matrices/ics/), [STIX](https://docs.oasis-open.org/cti/stix/v2.1/os/stix-v2.1-os.html), [TAXII](https://docs.oasis-open.org/cti/taxii/v2.1/os/taxii-v2.1-os.html), [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog).

## Enforce authorization and feasibility at execution time

The local command gate authenticates principal/delegation, authorizes exact asset/action and validates typed schema/units, capability, operating mode, topology/config/policy revision, fresh required measurements, feasibility, network/thermal/ramp/reserve/critical-load limits, reservations/locks and approved action hash. It enforces not-before/expiry, replay protection, logical idempotency and fencing immediately before adapter invocation. Decision receipts bind command, evidence, policy, model and approval digests. Missing policy, state or authorization blocks the dependent discretionary action.

Permitted commands pass to bounded adapters; ACK and observed readback are separate evidence. On a timeout the result is completionUnknown, not success or a blind retry. Compensation is a newly assessed action under current plant state. Planned switching, resynchronization, firmware/relay/protection changes require the applicable authorized human/utility procedure; the model cannot grant an emergency bypass. The [command proposal template](../../templates/command-proposal.json) is documentary, not an accepted API schema.

For each fixed critical rejection test, prove zero adapter execution and persisted denial reason. Threat reassessment must replay from pinned inputs/policy, and stale or unsigned inputs must not improve posture. Certificate revocation and withdrawn/expired approval take effect at execution, not only proposal creation. Simulation acceptance must demonstrate independent modeled protection and service fallback during total model/network loss. The package verifier checks artifacts only; none of these controls has been executed by this research.
