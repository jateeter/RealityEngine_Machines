## How should electricity carbon intensity be represented and verified?

### Takeaway
The domain needs separate, typed measures for consumed-electricity inventory intensity, dispatch-impact estimates and contractual reporting. Safety, reserve, service continuity and electrical constraints remain hard constraints; a model cannot lower apparent intensity by dropping critical loads, double-counting storage, or using an incompatible emissions series.

### Cited Findings
- ElectricityMaps describes its core signal as attributional, location-based and consumption-based using intergrid flow tracing, and distinguishes direct from lifecycle factors, preliminary/estimated data and forecast horizons. Therefore provider signal metadata must travel with each value; a country production average is not interchangeable with consumed mix. [ElectricityMaps methodology](https://www.electricitymaps.com/data/methodology), [API reference](https://app.electricitymaps.com/developer-hub/api/reference).
- WattTime distinguishes marginal emissions signals for action/dispatch from average signals for inventory. Access terms prohibit treating AOER as an operational load-shifting signal under the cited noncommercial agreement. [WattTime terms/methodology](https://watttime.org/noncommercial-agreement/).
- ENTSO-E publishes generation, load, transmission and balancing information under the European transparency framework and supplies a manual/data descriptions; the platform itself does not turn that input data into a universally agreed carbon intensity. [ENTSO-E Transparency Platform](https://www.entsoe.eu/data/transparency-platform/).
- GHG Protocol Scope2 covers purchased electricity/steam/heat/cooling and distinguishes contractual-instrument accounting with quality criteria. Its current guidance page still identifies the 2015 guidance and consultation completed January2026; pin adopted edition and treat proposed revisions as drafts unless formally released. [GHG Protocol Scope2](https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-2-guidance).

**Proposed quantitative contract:** store `numerator` (mass, gas basis CO2 vs CO2e/GWP vintage, direct/lifecycle scope), `denominator` (generated, imported, consumed or delivered kWh), geography/grid zone/site/boundary, timestamps/timezone/interval, source/method/version/vintage, average/marginal, location/market basis, observation/estimate/forecast/target, quality, uncertainty, measured coverage, freshness horizon and completeness. Compute interval energy-weighted intensity as total allocated emissions / delivered electrical energy over the same boundary/time; never arithmetic-average intensity over uneven-energy intervals. Zero denominator → undefined with reason, not zero.

Track direct fossil backup emissions and lifecycle PV/storage separately as chosen method requires. Storage has an energy inventory and associated charging-emissions inventory: charged kWh × same-boundary intensity plus allocated losses/auxiliaries, then allocate carried carbon to delivered discharge. Preserve SOC/time/source lineage. No free zero-carbon discharge; no double counting charge and discharge in one final-consumption total. Exported electricity and claimed avoided emissions remain separate ledgers unless approved accounting expressly allocates them. Marginal avoided-emissions estimates require a credible counterfactual, method, uncertainty, and no claim of measured absolute reduction just from a lower average signal. Distinguish owned-generation Scope1, purchased energy Scope2, lifecycle/supply-chain Scope3; market certificates do not alter physical flow-traced intensity. Keep target path, observed path and forecast path separate, report absolute emissions and service delivered alongside intensity, and stratify by operating mode (connected/islanded/emergency).

**Acceptance cases:** (a) lower-than-target intensity with rising energy still reports increased absolute emissions; (b) CO2 and CO2e series rejected for direct splice; (c) import-heavy zone differs from production intensity as expected; (d) energy weighting and unit conversions reproduce known totals; (e) loss-bearing storage does not erase carbon; (f) export not deducted twice; (g) stale/unavailable/estimated forecast downgrades quality and blocks unsupported carbon dispatch while safety control continues; (h) wrong region, interval, topology vintage or provider licensing scope is refused; (i) carbon proposal exceeding reserve/thermal/protection/critical-service envelope rejected; (j) revised data vintages preserve original dispatch receipt and regenerate audit reporting with a revision trail.

### Gaps
No regional provider has been selected, no site energy/carbon ledger exists, no external API account or terms acceptance is authorized here, and no target intensity curve is owner-approved. Actual numerical tolerances must be defined from selected provider uncertainty, calibrated metering and approved engineering criteria; do not invent a blanket emissions target or HIL pass threshold.
