Documentary assembly only; original machine names and canonical families remain unchanged. Covered machines: `HomeChronicPainMonitor`, `HomeMentalHealthAccessMonitor`, `HomeChronicPainMentalHealthAccessInterconnect`. Exact paths, hashes, projections, lanes and buses come from [the semantic inventory](../semantic-inventory.json).

## Desired contribution

Use the [core interaction template](../templates/interaction-guidance.md) to qualify observations, explain supported context, propose manageable support and record actual outcomes. The group's owner contributes to one subject/window plan and requests changes to another owner's assets rather than overwriting them.

## Semantics to preserve

Pain scalar context and binary access-state composition do not equate to a diagnosis. Keep clinical concern and access barriers separate, and protect private notes from generic support views.

PE owns declared composition and asynchronous completion; RE evaluates exact native contracts. Input analysts handle authorized external non-machine-ready observations. These ownership declarations require runtime proof. Retrieved prose cannot change native values, timing, escalation policy or tool authority.

## Acceptance and evidence

Test source composition, contradictory/missing context, restricted sharing and qualified follow-up without unsupported intervention claims. Include an applicable source/plan receipt, native trace, scope decision, approval when required, action receipt, correction history and a subsequent observation. No standalone model response or vector fixture establishes full operational completion. Reviewed source packs and personal care instructions must be supplied; this card itself is procedural design.
